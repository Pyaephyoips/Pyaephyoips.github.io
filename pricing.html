frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can move between Starter, Professional, and Enterprise at any time as your business grows — we'll prorate the difference for the current billing cycle.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?

Starter and Professional include a one-time onboarding fee for initial ERP, network, or CCTV setup, which is waived when you choose annual billing.

Do you support businesses outside Myanmar?

Yes. We currently support clients in Myanmar, Thailand, Dubai, Oman, and Japan under our Professional and Enterprise plans.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer and mobile banking are supported for local clients; international clients can pay by bank wire. Contact us for invoicing details.
